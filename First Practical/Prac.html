Kinky hair is the most delicate of hair types and hair textures. Often characterized by tight coils in a zig zag pattern, these strands require as much moisture they can get to prevent dryness, which often leads to hair breakage. To treat, care for and promote hair growth for this hair type & texture, it’s important to gently cleanse, detangle softly & moisturize with nourishing butters and oils.

Ideal Products to Use:

  • Detangle Me! Pre-Shampoo
  • Moisture Boost Me! Creamy Conditioner
  • Soften Me! Leave In Butter
  • Seal Me! Hair & Scalp Sealing Oil
